04 / KEEP YOUR SCIENTIST HAT ON

A few notes
before you conclude.

Good questions deserve careful answers.

01 A hot spot isn’t a whole wildfire.

Satellite detections depend on the instrument, cloud cover, overpass time, and fire intensity. One fire can generate many detections. The data explorer prefers VIIRS where available and falls back to MODIS; read the selected metric and sensor coverage before comparing years.

02 A missing reading isn’t a zero.

Water, air, and fishery measurements don’t cover every place and year. Empty values mean that a measurement is unavailable, not that nothing happened. The current calendar year can also be incomplete.

03 Correlation is a clue, not a cause.

Weather, river flow, habitat, fishing pressure, and the ocean also matter. Associations and before/after comparisons help form hypotheses; they cannot isolate wildfire as the cause of a salmon change.

04 Money and catch are different measures.

Canadian and US fishery values use different currencies (CAD and USD). Russia’s catch volume measures something different again. Compare the same metric within its context, rather than treating country totals as equivalent.
